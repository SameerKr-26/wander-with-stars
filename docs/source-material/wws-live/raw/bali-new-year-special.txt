Bali · Dec 26 – Jan 3, 2027 · 9 days · 1–24 guests

Welcome 2027 in true island style! Explore the tropical magic of Bali, Gili T & Nusa Penida, meet like-minded travellers, chase breathtaking sunsets, experience iconic adventures and celebrate New Year surrounded by your new travel tribe. ✨🏝️🥂

Hotel Check-In: Check in and relax after your flight.

Sunset: Watch the stunning sunset over the ocean.

Gili T Nightlife: Party the night away with your group at the island's popular night spots.
Overnight Stay: Gili Trawangan
Meals: Breakfast

Gili T to Bali & Finns Beach Club Experience | 30 Dec

Bali Leisure Day & New Year's Eve | 31 Dec

Meals: Breakfast

Welcome 2027, Tanah Lot, Uluwatu Sunset & Kecak Dance | 1 Jan
