About this Experience

Begin your journey in Ho Chi Minh City with a vibrant exploration of its cultural landmarks. Travel to Da Nang, known for its stunning beaches and iconic bridges, and take part in thrilling activities like a Coconut Basket Boat Ride and a visit to Ba Na Hills. Journey to the ancient lantern city of Hoi An for breathtaking views and local flavors. Cruise through Halong Bay, kayak amidst limestone islands, and discover Hanoi's unique train street. This trip offers an unforgettable blend of food, fun, and discoveries in Vietnam.
